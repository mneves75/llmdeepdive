Scope: bilingual Anatomy Explorer. Visitor mode: explore.

Audience: learners who need to connect named LLM components to their physical
role in a transformer block, then continue into the lesson that teaches each
mechanism.

Job and action: choose any component, locate it in the 3D specimen, inspect its
evidence and five lenses, optionally isolate its owning mechanism, then open the
resolved lesson.

Proof on hand: 12 real library components; bilingual technical facts; valid
build-resolved lesson links; keyboard and pointer selection; WebGL poster
fallback; deterministic procedural geometry; no runtime model asset.

Constraints: Three.js stays lazy and optional; all content works without WebGL;
every selector maps to a numbered port; input→output direction and layer
identity remain visible without the drawer; EN/pt-BR states stay equivalent;
desktop and mobile preserve selector → stage → evidence order.

Chosen direction: Signal Observatory, an expression of the Abyssal Core Atlas.
The specimen reads as a mature scientific instrument: machined decks, exposed
mechanisms, residual bypasses, luminous signal flow and restrained labels.

Chosen composition: a compact introduction above a horizontal component
selector, dominant abyssal stage and adjacent evidence drawer. The five lenses
form one ruled analytical strip below; mobile adds an explicit swipe affordance
to the selector before stacking the stage and drawer.
