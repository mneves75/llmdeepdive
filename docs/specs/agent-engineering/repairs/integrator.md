## Length-extreme floor (2026-10-19)

`content:parity` now requires, per track of ten or more items and per locale, that the correct option sit at a unique length extreme in at least 10% of items. Tracks 15–25 had none. In each track three items in three different lessons were repaired so the correct option is uniquely shortest (two items) or uniquely longest (one item) in both locales. A shortest repair tightens the correct option or makes a distractor more specific; a longest repair tightens the distractors that tied or exceeded it, since the correct option is never padded. No `correctIndex`, option count or option position changed, and no explanation needed new text. Options are lettered A–D in source order.
